<en-US>
New in 1.1.0

• Video stories: pick a moving background and a calm sound (rain, rivers, birds, mosque ambience, adhan and more), choose 10, 15 or 30 seconds, and share an MP4
• A new editor: text, style, video and sound tools, plus a + button to start a story from scratch
• Prayer alerts: adhan, sound, vibration or off, for each prayer
• Choose your country and city, or use your current location
• A next-prayer countdown in your notifications
