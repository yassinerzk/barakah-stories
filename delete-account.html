Delete your account

If you made a Barakah Stories account, you can delete it here. Sign in with the same email and password you use in the app.

This cannot be undone. Your account, your synced posts and your Quran reading progress are permanently deleted. Posts saved on your phone stay on your phone — uninstalling the app removes those.

Online accounts are not enabled for this build of the app, so there is nothing to delete here. If you believe you have an account, email info@klaycreate.com and we will handle it for you.

Prefer to do it in the app? Open Me → Account → Delete account.
